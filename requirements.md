# MetricMind - Business Requirements

## 1. Business Objective

MetricMind is designed to help business users get analytical
insights by asking questions in natural language.

The system will use a governed semantic layer to provide
consistent and reliable business metrics.

## 2. Target Users

- Business analysts
- Managers
- Business decision-makers
- Data teams

## 3. Key Business Metrics

### Revenue
Total income generated by the business.

### Cost
Total business cost associated with operations.

### Margin
The difference between Revenue and Cost.

## 4. Key Dimensions

### Time
Business results can be analyzed by time periods.

Examples:
- Day
- Month
- Quarter
- Year

### Geography
Business results can be analyzed by geographical areas.

Examples:
- Country
- State
- Region
- City

## 5. Example Business Questions

1. What is the total revenue?
2. What is the total cost?
3. What is the margin?
4. What is the revenue by month?
5. What is the revenue by geography?
6. Which geography has the highest revenue?
7. How does margin change over time?

## 6. Expected Output

MetricMind should provide:
- Business metric results
- Relevant dimensions
- Analytical insights
- Visualizations where applicable
- Transparent information about the query

## 7. Governance Requirement

Business metrics should be defined through a governed
semantic layer instead of allowing the AI agent to directly
query raw warehouse tables.
