# MetricMind Semantic Layer

## 1. Overview

The MetricMind semantic layer provides governed definitions of sales metrics and dimensions. It sits between the analytical data warehouse and the AI agent so that business questions use consistent metric definitions instead of generating arbitrary SQL.

**Objectives**
- Provide consistent business metric definitions.
- Enable analysis across time, geography, products, and customer segments.
- Support reliable analytical queries and business explanations.
- Document the approved metrics and dimensions used by the project.

## 2. Data Source

**Source table:** `METRICMIND.ANALYTICS.FCT_SALES`

The semantic model uses the transformed sales fact table as its analytical source.

## 3. Measures

| Measure | Aggregation or formula | Business meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `revenue` | Sum of `sales` | Total sales revenue |
| `profit` | Sum of `profit` | Total profit |
| `shipping_cost` | Sum of `shipping_cost` | Total shipping cost |
| `profit_margin` | `100.0 * profit / NULLIF(revenue, 0)` | Profit as a percentage of revenue |
| `quantity` | Sum of `quantity` | Total quantity of products sold |
| `discount` | Sum of `discount` | Total discount amount |
| `average_discount` | Average of `discount` | Average discount value per sales record |
| `average_selling_price` | `revenue / NULLIF(quantity, 0)` | Average revenue per unit sold |

### Metric Interpretation

- **Revenue:** Used to compare sales performance across regions, markets, categories, and time periods.
- **Profit:** Used to evaluate the amount of profit generated by sales.
- **Shipping cost:** Used to understand the cost of shipping products.
- **Profit margin:** Used to compare profitability relative to revenue. The result is expressed as a percentage.
- **Quantity:** Represents the total quantity of products sold.
- **Discount:** Represents the total value of the source `discount` field.
- **Average discount:** Represents the average value of the source `discount` field per sales record. Confirm the source field's units before interpreting it as a currency amount.
- **Average selling price:** Represents total revenue divided by total units sold, rather than the simple average of individual row prices.

The formulas above document the intended metric definitions. The actual Cube model remains the implementation source of truth.

## 4. Dimensions

Dimensions describe how measures can be grouped and filtered.

### Time
- `order_date` - Order date

### Geography
- `country` - Customer country
- `region` - Sales region
- `state` - Sales state
- `city` - Sales city
- `market` - Sales market

### Product
- `category` - Product category
- `sub_category` - Product sub-category
- `product_id` - Product identifier
- `product_name` - Product name

### Customer
- `segment` - Customer segment
